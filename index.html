Introducing

Agent Q

Agent Q is an AI assistant for regulated documentation in GxP and ISO environments. It works with any type of regulated information, from your approved procedures, forms and records to the applicable guidelines and standards such as EU GMP, PIC/S, ICH and ISO, and puts that knowledge at the fingertips of everyone who writes, reviews or relies on your documents.

Agent Q is delivered the way that suits you: as Software as a Service (SaaS) or locally on premise, on your company's own hardware.

What Agent Q does

These are the standard modules included with Agent Q. Thanks to its microkernel architecture, the existing modules can be adapted and additional modules added, so that Agent Q fully fits your company's needs.

01

Deviations & CAPA

Drafts deviation reports and CAPA plans field by field from the facts you enter, following your own procedures, and fills in your company's Word forms.

02

Change requests

Helps prepare change requests and proposes their GxP and regulatory classification, naming the criteria it relied on.

03

Compliance check of drafts

Checks a draft document section by section against regulations and standards before it goes to review, and lists proposed findings with the clause they refer to.

04

Guideline search

Answers questions from the official guidelines and standards with the exact passage, so there is no more scrolling through hundreds of pages.

05

Ask your own documents

Ask how a task is done or what was decided before, and get the answer from your own procedures and records, with the document it came from.

06

Private office assistant

A locally run language model helps you correct texts, draft e-mails and translate sensitive documents, without sharing them with AI companies.
