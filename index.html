The problem we're solving

Quality and compliance teams sit on hundreds of SOPs, forms, past deviation records, and regulatory guidelines — and spend real time just finding the right procedure, the right governing document, or the right regulatory clause. General-purpose AI tools aren't built for this: they can't be trusted with confidential documents, and they'll confidently answer even when they shouldn't.
